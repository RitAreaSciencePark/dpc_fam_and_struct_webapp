DPCstruct Metaclusters

⬡ Domain Classification in Structural Space
Properties CATH SCOP

The DPCstruct pipeline applied the Density Peak Clustering (DPC) algorithm to roughly 15 million representative AlphaFold2- predicted structures from the Foldseek Cluster database, producing 28,246 structural metaclusters without any manual annotation. Because protein structure is more conserved than sequence over evolutionary time, DPCstruct recovers remote homologs, including proteins sharing a common fold at less than 30% sequence identity, that purely sequence-based methods typically miss.

Use the toggle buttons to switch between the Properties view (intrinsic structural metrics and Pfam annotations), the CATH fold annotations, or the SCOP fold annotations. Click any MCID to open its detail page, where you can explore the sequences and launch the integrated PDBe-Mol* 3D viewer.

Legend
{% if view_type == 'properties' %}
  • A. Metacluster Structural Properties :
  • MCID: Unique identifier per metacluster.
  • Size: Number of structural domain members.
  • Avg. Len.: Mean domain length of all members (amino acids).
  • Len. Std: Standard deviation of domain lengths.
  • Len. Ratio: Ratio of standard deviation to average domain length.
  • pLDDT: Mean AlphaFold2 confidence score (predicted Local Distance Difference Test) across all members.
  • Disorder: Mean fraction of residues predicted as intrinsically disordered.
  • TM-Score: Mean intra-cluster pairwise alignment Template Modeling score, averaged over all pairs, using the maximum between TM-score normalized by query or target to account for size differences.
  • lDDT: Mean intra-cluster local Distance Difference Test score (local structural similarity).
  • pIdent: Mean intra-cluster sequence similarity (%).
  • B. Pfam Annotations:
  • Pfam Score: Global Pfam Score quantifying the agreement between the metacluster and Pfam domains.
  • Pfam Labels: Assigned Pfam labels (clans or families). Displayed as UNKNOWN (representing 'dark matter') if no Pfam domains are assigned to the metacluster.
{% elif view_type == 'cath' %}
  • Agreement between CATH Folds and DPCstruct Metacluster Domains:
  • CATH Fold: CATH fold identifier.
  • MCID: DPCstruct metacluster identifier.
  • DPC Target: Target DPCstruct protein domain.
  • Q./T.Range: Amino acid coordinate ranges for query and target.
  • Q./T.Cov: Coverage metrics indicating how much of each domain was covered in the alignment.
  • A.TM-Score: Template Modeling score of the structural alignment (0-1).
  • lDDT: Local Distance Difference Test score.
  • pIdent: Percent sequence identity between query and target.

  • Q./T.Range: Amino acid coordinate ranges for query and target.
  • Q./T.Cov: Coverage metrics indicating how much of each domain was covered in the alignment.
  • A.TM-Score: Template Modeling score of the structural alignment (0-1).
  • lDDT: Local Distance Difference Test score.
  • pIdent: Percent sequence identity between query and target.
